Prayer times

The times don’t match my mosque

Calculated times are estimates that depend on the method and your location. Open Journey › Me › Prayer times & location, check that your location is right, and choose the calculation method your community follows.

When in doubt, follow your trusted local timetable or mosque.

Reminders aren’t arriving

Open the Settings app, tap Notifications, then Wird, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Check that a Focus isn’t silencing Wird.

In Wird, open Journey › Me › Notifications & Devices to choose which reminders you get. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they keep working offline.

Times are off after travelling

Update your location in Journey › Me › Prayer times & location. Wird recalculates the times and reschedules your reminders.

Account and data

Do I need an account?

No. Without one, everything stays on your device. An account lets your records sync between your devices.

How do I delete my account?

Open Journey › Me › Account, then tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile and every record linked to your account, then erases Wird’s data on that device.

Not signed in? The same place offers Erase data on this device.

My records aren’t syncing

Make sure you are signed in to the same account on each device and connected to the internet. Anything you logged offline syncs once you reconnect.

Quran and recite back

Recite back asks me to download something

Recite back uses a Quran speech model that runs on your device. Wird downloads it once, with your permission, and shows the size first. After that, checking your recitation works without sending your voice anywhere.

Recite back marked a word I said correctly

It can happen in a noisy room or when reciting quickly. Recite back is a memorization aid: it does not grade tajweed and is not a religious ruling. Try again somewhere quieter, a little more slowly.

Widgets

How do I add a Wird widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Wird. Choose Prayers or Today’s Ayah. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a Wird widget.

Supporting Wird

How do I cancel a monthly gift?

Monthly support is an App Store subscription. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Wird and tap Cancel Subscription. Nothing in Wird changes when you do.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What Wird is, and isn’t

Not a source of rulings

Wird is a personal companion for your practice. It does not issue religious rulings. Please consult qualified scholars for religious questions.

Prayer times are estimates

Calculated prayer times depend on the method and your location. Follow your trusted local timetable or mosque.

Motivation, not merit

Droplets, the tree and milestones are tools for rhythm inside the app. They don’t represent religious reward or anyone’s standing with Allah.

Recite back is an aid

Recite back helps you memorize by marking words to review. It does not grade tajweed and is not a ruling on your recitation.

Supporting Wird

Wird is free. Optional support, monthly or once, is purchased through the App Store and handled by Apple. It never unlocks anything to do with worship. Monthly support renews until you cancel it in your Apple Account’s Subscriptions settings.

What Wird is, and isn’t

Not a source of rulings

Wird is a personal companion for your practice. It does not issue religious rulings, and neither does Ask: it quotes the Quran and hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim word for word, with the reference, and never speaks for them. Please consult qualified scholars for religious questions.

Prayer times are estimates

Calculated prayer times depend on the method and your location. Follow your trusted local timetable or mosque.

Motivation, not merit

Droplets, the tree and milestones are tools for rhythm inside the app. They don’t represent religious reward or anyone’s standing with Allah, and Wird never scores, ranks or compares anyone’s worship.

Recite back is an aid

Recite back helps you memorize by marking words to review. It does not grade tajweed and is not a ruling on your recitation.

Plans and payments

Everything that is worship is free for everyone: the five prayers with their reminders, the Quran reader, dhikr, the tree, the nightly check-in and your history, with no ads on any plan. Two paid plans add the layer around worship. Wird Plus is for one adult. Wird Family is for a household: everything in Plus for the parent, plus Wird Kids on up to five children’s iPads and the family garden. The current prices and the full list of what each plan includes are on the plans page; prices are in US dollars and the App Store shows your local currency.

Plans are bought through the App Store and handled by Apple. A monthly or yearly plan renews until you cancel it in your Apple Account’s Subscriptions settings; a free trial, where offered, converts to the paid plan at its end unless you cancel before then. A one-time purchase does not renew. Refunds are requested from Apple. If your family stops using Wird Family in its first 30 days, you can also write to us and we will refund the year.

Stop paying and nothing you did is taken away: every prayer, page and journal entry stays readable and exportable. Only the extras pause. We may change prices for new purchases; an existing subscription keeps its price unless you agree to a change.

Children and Wird Kids

Wird Kids is the companion for children, used on an iPad that a parent or guardian sets up and links to their own Wird. The child never sees a price, an advertisement, Ask or Together. The parent or guardian is responsible for the child’s use and may unlink or erase the child’s data at any time from inside Wird. Wird Kids is designed for Apple’s Kids Category and its rules.

Content and sources

The Arabic text of the Quran is the Tanzil Uthmani text, unmodified. Translations come from QuranEnc and are shown with the translator named. Hadith are cited from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim by reference. Everything in Discover is reviewed and comes from named sources. If you find an error in any text, write to us and we will check it against the source.

Together

Together lets adults share reflections and join small circles. You must be 18 or older to post. Posts are moderated: anyone can report a post, a person reviews every report, and we remove what breaks these rules. Do not post rulings, personal details of others, anything unlawful, or anything that mocks worship or people. We may remove posts and suspend posting without notice to protect others. Nobody sees who did not come to the water; circles show counts only.

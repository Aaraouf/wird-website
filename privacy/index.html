Privacy

Wird is a personal practice companion. It has no ads, no analytics or tracking from other companies, and no public counts of anything devotional.

This page is a short summary. The full privacy policy, the same one linked from the app and the App Store, has every detail and is the one that applies.

Read the full privacy policy

What stays on your device

  • Your exact location. It is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla, and is never sent to our servers. With an account, only a city name and time zone sync.
  • Your recitation. Recite back records only while you tap record. Your voice is analysed on your phone, held in memory for that check, then discarded. It is never saved or uploaded.
  • Your compass heading for the Qibla finder. It is not stored or sent.
  • Everything, if you don’t make an account. Without one, your records stay on your device.

What syncs if you make an account

An account is optional. With one, your records sync over an encrypted connection so you can use Wird on more than one device. Access rules let each account read only its own records.

Your Evening Journal syncs too, but it is only ever shown back to you. It is never used for insights, recommendations or analysis.

What Wird never does

  • Show ads, or sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Use tracking or analytics services from other companies.
  • Rank people, compare them, or judge anyone’s faith.
  • Rewrite or generate Quran, hadith or dua text.

Your choices

  • Delete your account from Journey › Me › Account › Delete account. This removes your profile and every record linked to it.
  • Erase data on this device from the same place when you are not signed in.
  • Turn off notifications or location at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.

Children

Wird is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

A gentle guide

Coming back to prayer

A lot of us had the good stretches and the slides. If you are reading this, you are already on your way back. This page is short on purpose.

Start with one prayer

Not five. One: the prayer your day has the most room for. For many people that is Maghrib or Isha. Pray it today, and let that be the whole plan. Tomorrow, the same one.

When a prayer passes, make it up and carry on

A missed prayer is not the end of anything. Pray it when you can and keep going. A hard week is a quiet week, not a reset, and coming back after one is the practice itself.

Leave the past years with someone qualified

What to do about the prayers you missed over the years is a question for a scholar or an imam you trust. It is separate from today's prayer. You do not have to settle one before you begin the other.

Add the next one when it feels light

When the first prayer has held for a week or two, add the one beside it. Later, a small root: a page of the Quran after a prayer you already keep, or a few minutes of dhikr.

Where Wird fits, if you want it

Wird keeps a gentle record. Each prayer you log waters a tree that grows over months. On quiet days its leaves dry a little. It never dies, and when you come back the leaves lift again. A passed prayer simply reads Make up. Nothing turns red, and nobody else sees it. All of this is free. A paper list with a tick for each return works just as well.

Return to rhythm, not perfection.

Questions

How do I start praying again after years away?

Start with one prayer that fits your day, and keep that one for a week or two before adding the next. A missed prayer along the way is made up when you can. It is not a reset.

What about the prayers I missed in the past?

Ask a scholar or an imam you trust. That question is separate from today's prayer, and you do not need to settle it before you begin again. Wird does not count past prayers or rule on them.

Is Wird free for this?

Yes. The five prayers and their reminders, the Quran, dhikr and your tree are free for everyone, with no ads.

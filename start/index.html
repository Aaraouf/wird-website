How Wird handles your data

  • Your exact location stays on your phoneIt is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla. With an account, only a city name syncs.

  • Your recitation never leaves your deviceIt is checked on your phone, then discarded. It is never saved or uploaded.

Questions

Is Wird free?

Yes, and it always will be. You can choose to support Wird monthly or once through the App Store. Support never unlocks anything to do with worship.

What happens when I miss a prayer?

Nothing breaks. A passed prayer reads Make up, and you can log it whenever you pray it. On quiet days your tree grows thirsty, but it never dies, and it recovers quickly when you come back.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account, everything stays on your device. An account lets your records sync between devices, and you can delete it, with everything linked to it, from inside the app.

How are prayer times worked out?

On your phone, from your location and a calculation method you can change. Your exact location is never sent to our servers.

Does Wird judge my recitation?

No. Recite back compares what you say with the text and marks words you may want to review. It does not grade tajweed and is not a religious ruling.

Where does the Quran text come from?

The Arabic is the Tanzil Uthmani text, unmodified. Translations come from QuranEnc. Everything in Discover is reviewed and comes from named sources.

Which devices does Wird run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. Prayers and Today’s Ayah widgets work on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Is my data sold or shared?

Never. Wird has no ads and no tracking or analytics from other companies. Your records are used to run Wird for you, never for advertising or public rankings.

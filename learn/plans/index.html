Wird plans: what is free, what Plus and Family cost

A prayer app free of ads and free of a paywall on worship is what most people in this category are asking for, and it is the thing most apps in the category do not give them. Wird by wirdwellness.app is built on one rule: worship is never behind a price. This page says what that rule covers, what the two paid plans add, and what they cost, in the same words the app uses on its own paywall.

Last updated 29 October 2026. Wird is coming to the App Store; prices are in US dollars and the App Store shows your local currency.

If you only want the numbers: the five prayers, the tree, the whole Qur'an reader, dhikr and your history are free for everyone, always. Wird Plus is $4.99 a month, $39.99 a year, or $49.99 once. Wird Family is $6.99 a month or $59.99 a year with a 14-day trial. Nothing is taken away if you stop paying. There are no ads on any plan.

Wird is coming to the App Store. The plans below are the ones it will launch with.

What is free in Wird?

Everything that is worship is free in Wird, for everyone, for good: the five prayers with their reminders, the tree that grows when you log them, dhikr, the nightly check-in, the whole Qur'an reader with a named translation, Wird Kids on one iPad, and your history. No ads on any plan. Paid plans add the layer around worship, never worship itself.

Why a page like this exists

Most prayer apps have a plans page that lists what you get when you pay. This one starts with what you get when you do not. That order is on purpose.

The most common complaint in this category is paying to worship. Muslim Pro keeps a help article titled Is it acceptable to pay for a religious application?, which tells you how often the question comes up. Reviews of Daleel AI and Quranly carry the same objection in different words: a hadith behind a paywall, a habit feature that asks whether you really cannot afford it.

Wird's answer is to draw the line in a place you can check. Below the line is the practice. Above it are conveniences, community features and things that cost money per use. You pay for the second group or you do not, and either way you can pray, read and grow the tree.

The paywall inside the app lists what is free in the same words as this page. If the two ever disagree, write to hello@wirdwellness.app and this page is the one that is wrong.

Where the line is drawn, and why

Free is the worship itself and nothing that merely surrounds it. You can log every prayer, read the whole Qur'an, keep dhikr, grow your tree and keep your history without paying a cent, and the app will never ask you to.

Plus is the layer around the practice. Widgets beyond the first one, the Fajr alarm, posting in Together, more Ask questions, insights, memorisation tracking. Each of these is a convenience, a community feature, or something that costs Wird money every time it is used.

Family is Plus for the parent, plus the children. Wird Kids on one iPad is free so a family can see what it is. Family links up to five iPads to the parent's phone, adds the family garden, the prophets' stories as they are released, Juz Amma packs and family challenges.

Three things to notice. Plus and Family give the adult the same extras; Family only adds the children. A one-child family can stay on the free Kids iPad for as long as they like. And Family is sold to the parent, never to the child: the child's iPad never shows a price.

What a plan pays for

A fair question, and one that deserves a plain answer. Money from Plus and Family goes to three things.

  • Hosting and sync. Together, the family garden and your synced history run on servers that cost money every month.
  • Ask. Each question in Ask is answered by a language model that quotes the Qur'an and Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim word for word. Every answer has a per-question cost, which is why the free allowance is small and the paid one is large.
  • Review. Content in Discover and Wird Kids is reviewed before release. The stories for children are released one at a time, each reviewed first.

Nothing from a plan goes to advertising. Wird has no acquisition budget at launch; its channels are the App Store page, Instagram, and people telling each other.

The promises that come with a plan

These hold on every plan, and they are the ones to hold Wird to.

  1. Worship is never behind a price. Nobody pays to log a prayer, read the Qur'an or grow their tree.
  2. Nothing is taken away. Stop paying and every prayer, page and journal entry stays readable and exportable. Only the extras pause.
  3. Cancel in one tap in iPhone Settings. Apple handles it, not Wird. If a family stops using it in the first 30 days, ask and the year is refunded.
  4. No ads, ever, on any plan. Not at adhan time, not in the Qur'an, not anywhere.
  5. No discounts. The price on this page is the price. The only concession is the free trial.
  6. The paywall says what is free, in these words, so nobody feels tricked into paying for something they already had.

Objections, answered plainly

"A prayer app should not have a subscription at all." A lot of us feel that way, and the free plan is built for exactly that position: it is the whole practice, not a sample of it. The subscription pays for the parts that cost money to run, and you can live in Wird for years without touching it.

"I would rather pay once." You can. Wird Plus is $49.99 once, any time of year. Family can be bought once for $149.99, but only between Ramadan and Eid 2027.

"What happens if Wird shuts down or I stop paying?" The same thing either way for your data: it stays yours. Your history is exportable from inside the app, and nothing you logged is locked behind a lapsed plan.

"Is it even acceptable to pay for a religious app?" Wird does not give rulings, on this or anything else. What Wird can say is what the money does and does not touch: it never touches the worship, and it pays for hosting, the per-question cost of Ask, and review. Whether that sits right with you is yours to decide, and the free plan is there if it does not.

"Will Family really replace the kids' app I already pay for?" It depends on what you pay for. If you pay for a video library, Family is not that; it is a prayer and Qur'an habit for the child, plus stories released one at a time. Frequently asked questions

How much does Wird Plus cost?

Wird Plus is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial, or $49.99 once with no renewal. It is for one adult and adds the full widget set, the Fajr alarm, posting and hosting in Together, Ask without the daily cap, insights, a yearly review and memorisation tracking. Prices are in US dollars.

How much does Wird Family cost, and what does it include?

Wird Family is $6.99 a month or $59.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial. It includes everything in Plus for the parent, plus Wird Kids on up to five iPads linked to the parent's phone, the family garden, prophets' stories told without faces, Juz Amma practice packs and family challenges. A one-time Family purchase at $149.99 is sold only from Ramadan to Eid 2027.

Is Wird free to use for prayer?

Yes. The five prayers with their reminders, the tree, dhikr, the nightly check-in, the whole Qur'an reader and your history are free for everyone with no ads and no time limit. Paid plans add conveniences and the family layer; they never add worship.

Which Muslim prayer app has no ads at all?

Wird has no ads on any plan, free or paid. Among established apps, Pillars is free with no ads and sells only cosmetic extras. Muslim Pro and Athan Pro show ads in their free tiers and sell ad removal; their listed prices are in the table on this page.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted and nothing is locked. Every prayer, Qur'an page and journal entry stays readable and exportable; only the extras pause. You cancel in iPhone Settings, and Apple processes it. A family that stops using Family in the first 30 days can ask for the year back.

Does Wird have a lifetime plan?

Wird Plus can be bought once for $49.99 at any time. Wird Family, forever, is the same Family plan for $149.99 once, sold only in the window from Ramadan to Eid 2027. Outside that window Family is monthly or yearly.

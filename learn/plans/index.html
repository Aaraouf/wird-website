Questions

Is Wird free?

Yes, for everything that is worship. The five prayers and their reminders, the Quran reader, dhikr, your tree and your history are free for everyone, with no ads.

What do the plans add?

Wird Plus adds the extras for one adult: the full set of widgets, the Fajr alarm, posting and circles in Together, Ask without the daily limit, insights and memorisation tools. Wird Family adds the household: everything in Plus for you, and Wird Kids on up to five children's iPads.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing you did is taken away. Every prayer, page and journal entry stays yours to read and export. Only the extras pause.

How do I cancel?

In one tap, in your iPhone's Settings under Subscriptions. Apple handles it.

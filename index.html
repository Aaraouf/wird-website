The Quran

Read it, hear it, keep it.

One reader for everything: the Uthmani text, a translation beside it, and a reciter you can follow verse by verse.

  • Listen and follow. Each verse lights up as it is recited, with repeat and speed controls.
  • Recite back. Recite from memory and Wird marks the words to review. Your voice is checked on your phone and never uploaded. It does not grade tajweed and is not a religious ruling.
  • Memorize steadily. Reviews come back at the right time, so what you learn stays.

Your worship is yours.

No ads, no outside trackers, no public counts of anything devotional. Sharing shows growth, never worship.

How Wird handles your data
  • Location stays on your phone. Exact coordinates are used for prayer times and the Qibla, never sent to us.
  • Recitation is checked on device. Held in memory for the check, then discarded.
  • Your journal is only for you. It never feeds insights, recommendations or analysis.
  • No account needed. If you make one, you can delete it and everything linked to it from inside the app.

Questions

What is Wird?

Wird is a calm daily companion for the five prayers, the Quran and small daily habits, for iPhone and iPad. Every prayer you log sends a drop of water to a tree that grows over months. On quiet days it waits for you. It never dies, and nothing turns red.

Is Wird free?

Everything that is worship is free for everyone: the five prayers with their reminders, the Quran reader, dhikr, the tree and your history, with no ads on any plan. Wird Plus ($4.99 a month, $39.99 a year, or $49.99 once) adds the extras for one adult. Wird Family ($6.99 a month or $59.99 a year, with a 14-day trial) covers a household with up to five children's iPads. Nothing is taken away if you stop paying.

Which devices does Wird run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. Wird Kids, the companion for children, runs on iPad. Wird is coming to the App Store.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account, everything stays on your device. An account lets your records sync between devices, and you can delete it, with everything linked to it, from inside the app.

Does Wird give religious rulings?

No. Ask quotes the Quran and hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim word for word, with the reference, and never speaks for them. For rulings, ask a qualified scholar.
